From your SFI AGC Unit

Around Vellayani3
A · Close to campusVellayani

The lake and its neighbours

  • Vellayani LakeMap ↗The only freshwater lake in the district, about 7.5 acres. Boating and birdwatching. Go early morning or evening. The Ayyankali boat race is held here at Onam.
  • Kireedam PaalamMap ↗The small bridge made famous by the 1989 film Kireedam. A favourite photo spot.
  • Vellayani Devi TempleMap ↗A Kali temple about 700 years old. The Kaliyoottu Mahotsavam is held once every three years.
  • Birds of the wetlandMap ↗Seasonal visitors include citrine wagtail, amur falcon and spot-bellied pelican.
Around Vellayani4
B · Day tripsThe coast

Beaches and the coast

  • Kovalam Beach~13 kmMap ↗The classic. Lighthouse Beach, Hawa Beach and Samudra Beach sit along one curve of coast.
  • Vizhinjam Lighthouse~14 kmMap ↗Climb for a long view over the coast and the fishing harbour.
  • Kerala Arts & Crafts VillageMap ↗Handicrafts and artisans at work, close to Kovalam.

Swim only where lifeguards are on duty and stay out of the water on red flag days.

Around Vellayani5
B · Day tripsGreen escapes

Green escapes

  • Kuzhipallam Botanical GardenMap ↗A green stop for agriculture students who like to see plants outside the lab.
  • Poovar~30 kmMap ↗Estuary, sandbar beach and backwater boats at the southern edge of the district.
  • Neyyar Dam~30 kmMap ↗Reservoir, boating and the Lion Safari Park, with the Western Ghats behind.

Start early for Poovar and Neyyar. Each is a half day trip from campus.

Around Vellayani6
C · More placesNearby

Close to the city

  • Punchakkari~3 km from CentralMap ↗Canals and paddy fields about 3 km from the railway station. Boating, tea shops and evening walks. Skip the afternoon heat.
  • AruvippuramMap ↗Riverside temple on the Neyyar, where Sree Narayana Guru consecrated a Shiva idol in 1888, a landmark of Kerala’s social reform.
  • Manaveeyam VeedhiMap ↗A cultural street in the city with open air performances, exhibitions and evening gatherings.
Around Vellayani7
D · HillsWestern Ghats

Hills and trekking

  • Ponmudi~55 kmMap ↗The nearest hill station, in the Western Ghats about 55 km from the city. Hairpin bends, mist and cool air.
  • Bonacaud~61 kmMap ↗Forest and tea estate country. The Bonacaud picket station, about 61 km from the city, is the start of the Agasthyarkoodam trek.
  • AgasthyakoodamMap ↗A 1,868 m peak. The trek is 27 km over two days, in a short season around January and February. 70 trekkers a day, register online with the Forest Department.

Check dates and permits with the Forest Department first.

Around Vellayani8
D · HillsBeyond

More to explore

  • Varkala~50 kmMap ↗Cliff beach with cafes on the edge and the Janardhana Swami temple. About 50 km north of the city.
  • AmbooriMap ↗A hill village in the Vellarada side of the district. Koonichimala, the trek on the next page, is near it.

Ask locals for the best way to reach Amboori from the main road.

Around Vellayani9
D · HillsOffbeat treks

Offbeat hill treks

  • KoonichimalaMap ↗A hidden gem near Amboori, surrounded by lush greenery and misty hills. The trek rewards you with views of the Western Ghats and nearby reservoirs.
  • KurishumalaMap ↗Near Vellarada, a scenic hill known for its religious significance and peaceful surroundings. An uphill trek away from the busy city.
  • KondakettimalaMap ↗An offbeat hill near the Kerala–Tamil Nadu border, with rugged trails, dense greenery and panoramic views.

Go in a group, carry water and ask locals about access before you set out.

E · CityThiruvananthapuram

Landmarks in the city

  • Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple~11 kmMap ↗The best known landmark of the city. A strict dress code applies, so check it before you go.
  • Napier Museum and Zoo~12 kmMap ↗A museum of striking design in a large green compound with the zoo and gardens.
  • Shankumugham Beach~15 kmMap ↗An evening beach beside the airport, good for sunsets and a walk.

What to eat

  • Puttu and kadala curryThe standard Trivandrum breakfast.
  • Appam with ishtuLacy rice appam with a mild coconut stew.
  • SadyaThe banana leaf feast. Look for it at Onam and on festival days.

  • Karimeen and chemmeen curryPearl spot fish and prawn, cooked the Kerala way.

H · FoodFavourites

Old city favourites

  • Rahmaniya HotelMap ↗Chalai Bazaar, open since 1949. Known for chicken and lime juice. Arrive early.
  • The Travancore HotelMap ↗Travancore home-style dishes, vegetarian and non-vegetarian.
  • Indian Coffee HouseMap ↗Thampanoor. Cheap tea and snacks in a spiral building by Laurie Baker.
  • Villa MayaMap ↗A restored 18th century Dutch manor serving Kerala dishes.
  • Vismaya HotelMap ↗A local favourite for porotta and curry.
  • Katachelkuzhi Nadan Chicken CurryMap ↗Home-style Kerala chicken curry, the nadan way.

J · Good to knowTips

Before you go

  • KAU Crop MuseumSeeds and saplings are on sale at the university. Visits need permission and run 10 am to 4 pm on working days.
  • TemplesFollow the dress code and the photography rules at each one.
  • Keep the lake cleanIt supplies drinking water and irrigation. Take your waste back with you.
  • Hills and forestsGo in a group, carry water and follow Forest Department rules.
